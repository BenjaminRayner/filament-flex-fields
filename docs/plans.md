## Compare the scope

| | Single Product | Unlimited Company | Enterprise Group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hosted products | One named product | All licensee-owned products | All qualifying group-owned products |
| Licensee coverage | One person or legal entity | One entity or sole proprietor | Named parent + qualifying 100%-owned subsidiaries |
| Users, tenants and developers | Unlimited within scope | Unlimited within scope | Unlimited within scope |
| Servers and environments | Included within scope | Included within scope | Included within scope |
| Private modifications and application branding | Included within scope | Included within scope | Included within scope |
| Mandatory renewal to continue licensed use | None | None | None |
| Customer installations, OEM and redistribution | Separate Custom rights | Separate Custom rights | Separate Custom rights |
| SLA, custom development or other packages | Not included | Not included | Not included |

Enterprise Group expands entity coverage. It does not add OEM rights. A client, sister company or partly owned subsidiary is not automatically covered by a company license.

## Which plan fits your application?

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="One hosted product" icon="package">
    **Single Product is sufficient** for one named hosted product, even with many developers or customers. Multiple regions, tenant instances in your controlled hosting and pricing tiers can belong to the same product.

    A separately offered application is another product. Retiring a product does not turn its license into a reusable slot.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A product portfolio" icon="building-2">
    **Unlimited Company covers future products of the same entity.** At current list prices, two Single Product licenses cost $498, while three cost $747. Unlimited Company costs $699, making it less expensive from three eligible products onward.

    This comparison assumes the same entity owns and operates every product. It does not extend a company's license to its clients or subsidiaries.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A corporate group" icon="network">
    **Enterprise Group covers the named parent and qualifying wholly owned subsidiaries.** Ownership interests and voting rights must be 100% throughout the qualifying chain.

    Joint ventures, minority-owned companies and companies with a shared founder but no qualifying parent chain need separate or negotiated coverage. Keep the covered-entity list current; report changes within 30 calendar days.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Customer delivery" icon="truck">
    **Request Custom terms** for your reusable product installed in a customer's cloud or on-premises, server source or container delivery, starter kits, OEM and independent reseller rights.

    Managing the customer's installation yourself does not make it hosted use under a Standard Plan. Genuine bespoke development under a client's own direct grant has a separate [agency route](/docs/licensing-faq#agencies-and-client-work).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Upgrade without buying the same scope twice

Standard Plans originally issued under License v2.0 have **no upgrade deadline**. Eligible net license fees already paid can be credited toward the then-current destination price, with a minimum payable amount of zero.

| Upgrade | Additional license fee at current full list prices, before tax |
|---|---|
| One Single Product → Unlimited Company | $450 |
| Two eligible Single Product grants for the same entity → Unlimited Company | $201 |
| One Single Product → Enterprise Group | $1,250 |
| One Unlimited Company → Enterprise Group | $800 |

Credits exclude taxes, support, refunds, chargebacks and Custom fees. They have no cash value and cannot be used twice. Consolidation requires scope eligibility, affected holders' consent and a new confirmation identifying the replaced grants. Pre-v2.0 and Custom purchases receive an individual written quote.

## Request a license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your scope">
    Send the intended licensee's legal name, billing country and email, applicable company or tax identifier, requested plan and a short description of your application. For Single Product, include its name and URL if available; for Enterprise Group, include the parent and qualifying subsidiaries.

    Explain who owns the hosting account, whether customers receive only runtime access, and whether there is any source delivery, customer installation, reseller model or kit distribution. Include existing license references for upgrades or transfers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the quote before payment">
    Email [barek122@gmail.com](mailto:barek122@gmail.com). The scope, terms and price are agreed before you receive a payment link. Standard payments use Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record; Custom payment arrangements follow the quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your License Confirmation">
    After confirmed payment, the Copyright Holder issues or authenticates the grant. Commercial rights begin when both payment and the authenticated grant requirements are satisfied, unless expressly agreed otherwise. Keep the confirmation and receipt together; a tax receipt alone is not the license grant.
  </Step>
</Steps>
