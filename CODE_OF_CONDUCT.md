# Code of conduct

FLEX Fields welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds
and experience levels. Everyone taking part in project discussions, issues, pull
requests and other project spaces is expected to follow these rules, including
maintainers.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the work, explain your reasoning and give
  feedback that helps someone improve it.
- Be patient with questions, language differences and mistakes. Accept feedback
  and correct misunderstandings in good faith.
- Respect people's privacy, boundaries and time. Share only information you have
  permission to disclose.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and allow others to participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.
Neither are publishing someone's private information without permission, spam
or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting a concern

Email [barek122@gmail.com](mailto:barek122@gmail.com) with the subject
**FLEX Fields — conduct report**. Include what happened, where it happened and
any relevant links or evidence. Explain any immediate concerns and whether you
have a preferred way to be contacted. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

Reports are handled privately, with information shared only as needed to review
the concern and take action. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
If your report concerns a maintainer, identify that conflict so it can be
considered when deciding who reviews it. If it concerns the listed contact,
this project currently has no independent reporting contact.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may remove or edit disruptive content, close or lock discussions,
issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. They
consider the seriousness, context, impact and repetition of the behavior;
serious incidents may require an immediate restriction.

Where appropriate, the person affected by a moderation decision receives an
explanation and can request reconsideration by emailing the contact above with
relevant context. Maintainers should disclose conflicts of interest and involve
another uninvolved maintainer when one is available.
